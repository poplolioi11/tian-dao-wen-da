# 天道問答（DRS 428685358） / Tian dao wen da: Transcription Dataset

底本為哈佛燕京圖書館藏本 DRS 428685358，索書號 RAREB TA 1980.2 2470.2。題葉記光緒十五年己丑（1889）及「山東濟南府藏板」，館目著錄濟南救世會。 [館目](https://id.lib.harvard.edu/alma/990094312740203941/catalog) · [固定掃描](https://commons.wikimedia.org/w/index.php?title=File%3AHarvard_drs_428685358_%E5%A4%A9%E9%81%93%E5%95%8F%E7%AD%94.pdf&oldid=1293876334) · [館藏 IIIF](https://iiif.lib.harvard.edu/manifests/view/drs:428685358)

《天道問答》是一卷署名秀華陽的晚清官話基督教教理問答，現存底本刊於光緒十五年（1889），題葉記山東濟南府藏板。全書從人的由來問起，以「我今天」起首的連續提問引導每日自省，並談禱告、十誡的解說、免罪得福及救恩。這些問答呈現晚清濟南刊本如何以日常語言說明基督教教理與生活實踐，可供研究地方出版、傳教史與漢語宗教語彙。本資料集以哈佛燕京藏本 DRS 428685358 為底本，共取 22 幅固定影像，其中 14 幅用於轉錄正文，提供 JSON、TXT、TEI 和逐項覆核紀錄。此版為 AI 校讀稿，163 項比對中原有 31 項未解。補充回圖已釐清其中 3 項，仍有 28 項待確認。人工全文二校待完成。

Tian dao wen da is a one-volume late Qing Christian catechism in vernacular Mandarin attributed to Xiu Huayang. The source copy is dated 1889 (Guangxu 15), and the title leaf identifies Jinan Prefecture, Shandong, as the place where the printing blocks were held. The catechism opens by asking where human beings come from and uses a sequence of questions beginning “Today I…” to guide daily self-examination. It also discusses prayer, the meaning of the Ten Commandments, forgiveness, blessings and salvation. Its question-and-answer format offers material for studying local publishing, Christian missions and Chinese religious vocabulary in late Qing Jinan. This dataset uses 22 fixed images from Harvard-Yenching Library DRS 428685358. The main text is transcribed from 14 pages. The AI-reviewed JSON, TXT and TEI transcription records 31 unresolved comparison items from the primary review. Supplemental image review resolved 3 of those items, leaving 28 open. Full human collation is outstanding.

序末原印署「英教士秀華陽著」。前書衣另有英文手記，涉及據 Richard、Jones 的教理問答改寫增補，並載 1889 字樣。前本的確切題名及手書者身分待考。 [哈佛館目](https://id.lib.harvard.edu/alma/990094312740203941/catalog) · [固定原圖](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Special:PermanentLink/1293876334)

## 資料與校讀

- data/document.json 保留歷史文字、原圖位置、疑讀與缺口。
- data/text.txt 與 data/text.tei.xml 為全文與 TEI，附文字單位的對應檔。
- data/review.json 保留 163 項比對、31 項原裁定未解、補充回圖後仍待確認的 28 項及採用紀錄。
- data/manifest.json 與 PACKAGE-SHA256SUMS.txt 記錄固定發行的檔案雜湊。

固定底本 22 幅。p001 為題簽，p003 為題葉及刊記，p004 為題辭，p005–p006 為序與原印署款，p007–p020 為問答正文。p005–p020 可辨版心另列。襯葉、後封、透印及手寫記號依圖面所見註記。

The fixed witness comprises 22 images. The cover label is on p001, the title leaf and imprints on p003, the prefatory statement on p004, the preface and printed attribution on p005–p006, and the questions and answers on p007–p020. Legible running titles and folio numbers on p005–p020 are recorded separately. Endpapers, the back cover, show-through and handwritten marks are described as visible.

初錄與第二路讀稿各以同一批固定原圖為據。Claude 完成第 1–22 幅初錄與第 1–22 幅第二路讀稿。Claude 依原圖覆核 163 項比對。程式列出的 163 項比對均有逐項紀錄。採入 8 項有影像依據的更正，保留未解位置與候選。另有 24 項異讀保留原讀及候選。程式驗收確認公開 JSON、TXT 和 TEI 的逐欄內容一致。TEI 通過 P5 4.12.0 Relax NG。人工全文二校、專家審定和準確率量測待完成。

The two independent readings used the same fixed source images. Claude produced the first reading for images 1–22 and the second reading for images 1–22. Claude reviewed 163 comparison items against the images. The dataset records 163 comparison items. The edition incorporates 8 corrections supported by the images. The initial readings and alternatives remain recorded for 24 further proposed changes. Automated validation confirmed that the transcription content agrees column by column across JSON, TXT and TEI. The TEI passed P5 4.12.0 Relax NG validation. Full human collation, expert assessment and accuracy measurement are outstanding.
